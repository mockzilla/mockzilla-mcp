# Request top-ups

A top-up is extra requests on top of what your plan allows for the month. It is what to reach for when a month turns busier than you planned, instead of moving to a bigger plan for a spike that will not happen again.

Buying and reading them is owner-only, like the rest of Billing.

You will find them under **Billing**, on the **Request top-ups** tab. The **Top up** link on the Requests card above your simulations list opens the same dialog.

> You do not need a paid plan. A one-time top-up needs no subscription behind it, so any organization can buy one, free plans included.

## Two kinds

- **One-time**: pay once, and the requests last 90 days from the day you buy them.
- **Recurring**: the same amount comes back every month with your subscription, so the balance refills each billing period instead of running down to nothing. This one needs a subscription to attach to.

## Buy one

Choose **Add requests**.

(Image: The buy dialog: one-time or recurring, a quantity in units of 100,000 requests, the total, and a box to tick before paying.)

*Pick a kind and a size, tick the box, pay.*

Requests come in units of 100,000. Set the quantity and the total updates as you go.

Tick the box to confirm you have read what a plan change does, then **Confirm and pay**.

A one-time top-up goes through checkout, and the requests arrive once the payment clears. A recurring one joins the subscription you already have and shows up on your next invoice.

## What you have

The tab lists everything you have bought, active ones first.

(Image: The Request top-ups tab: four top-ups with their size, kind, status, price, purchase date and expiry, above the note that they are non-refundable.)

*Size, kind, status, what you paid, and when it runs out.*

- **Active**: counting toward what you can serve.
- **Expired**: a one-time top-up past its 90 days.
- **Forfeited**: lost to a plan change.
- **Cancelled**: stopped before it ran out.

The line above the table adds up how many are active and what the recurring ones come to each month.

## What gets used first

Requests come out of your plan's monthly allowance first. Top-ups start draining only once that is gone, soonest to expire first, so nothing sits unused while something newer is spent.

See Usage and limits (topic `simulations/usage-and-limits`).

## What a plan change does

> Top-ups are not refundable, and moving to a different plan forfeits every active one. Switching between monthly and yearly billing on the same plan keeps them.

You get told before it happens. The app names how many top-ups you would lose, how many requests that is, and what you paid for them, then asks you to confirm.

See Plans and limits (topic `billing/plans-and-limits`).
