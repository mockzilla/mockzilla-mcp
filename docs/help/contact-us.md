# Contact us

The contact form is how you reach us without an account. Use it to ask about
pricing for a team, report a problem with the website, or find out about using
Mockzilla in a course.

## The contact form

Open [Contact](https://mockzilla.org/contact) from the site header and fill in four fields.

- **Name** and **Email** tell us who to reply to.
- **Company** is optional.
- **Message** is what you want to ask.

(Image: The contact form with name, email, an optional company field and a message, above the Send Message button)

*The form takes a name, an email address, an optional company and your message.*

Click **Send Message**. We answer by email.

## Write to us directly

Two addresses go straight to the people who handle them.

- **education@mockzilla.org** for teachers, TAs and CS clubs. Classroom access and shared course simulations.
- **privacy@mockzilla.org** for privacy questions and data access requests.

## Tell us about a docs page

Every page here ends with **Was this page helpful?**. Answer yes or no, and add
a comment if something is missing or wrong. It goes to the people who write
these pages.

With an account, open a support issue instead. The thread keeps your
screenshots and our answers together, and everyone in your organization can
read it.

See Get support (topic `help/get-support`).
