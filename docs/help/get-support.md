# Get support

Support is where you report a bug, ask for a feature, or get help with
something that is not behaving. Each issue is a conversation with our team, and
it stays in your workspace.

Open it from **Support** in the sidebar.

## Open an issue

Click **New issue** and fill in the form.

- **Subject** is what the issue is called in the list. One line.
- **Category** routes it. Pick from Simulations, Resilient backends, Specs and endpoints, Deployment, Billing, Account and access, or Something else.
- **Simulation** is optional. Pick one and we can see which deployment you mean without asking.
- **What's happening?** is the description. Tell us what you expected and what happened instead.

(Image: The new issue form with a subject filled in, and the fields for category, an optional simulation, the description and images to attach)

*The form asks for a subject, a category, an optional simulation, and what happened.*

Attach images to show us what you are looking at. Paste or drop a screenshot
straight into the box, or click **Attach images**. Up to 5 images per message,
1 MB each, in PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP.

Click **Send to support** and the issue appears in your list with a number, like
`#1042`. Quote that number if you write to us about it anywhere else.

## Who can read it and who can reply

An issue belongs to your organization, not to the person who opened it. Everyone
in the organization reads the same thread, so a colleague can pick up an issue
you opened while you are away.

- **Viewers** read issues.
- **Editors, admins and owners** open issues and reply to them.

## Where an issue stands

Every issue carries a status, and the list shows it beside the number.

- **With support** means it is with us. A new issue starts here.
- **Your turn** means we have answered and are waiting on you.
- **Closed** means it is finished. We close issues; you never have to.

Closed issues are hidden until you click **Show closed**.

(Image: The issue list with three issues: one marked Your turn, one With support, and one Closed, each with its number and the time of the last message)

*The list shows each issue's number, status and the time of the last message.*

## Reply and reopen

Open an issue to read the whole thread. Our replies carry a **Support** badge, so
you can tell at a glance who wrote what. Write in the box at the bottom and click
**Send**.

(Image: One issue open: its number, status and subject at the top, the customer's report and the reply from support below it, and the reply box at the bottom)

*An open issue: the report, our answer, and the box for replying.*

Replying to a closed issue opens it again. One problem stays one thread, so you
do not have to start a new issue when a fix does not hold.

Without an account, write to us through the contact form instead.

See Contact us (topic `help/contact-us`).
