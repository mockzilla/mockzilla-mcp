# Members and invitations

Two tabs in Settings cover your team. **Members** lists the people who are in the organization. **Invitations** covers the ones you have asked to join. Owners and admins can use both; everyone else gets a note instead.

(Image: The Members tab: four people with their roles and joining dates, and a Remove action per row.)

*The Members tab*

Each row shows a role and the date that person joined. Your own row is marked **(you)**.

## Change a role

Pick a new role from the dropdown in the **Role** column. It applies straight away.

- Only an owner can make someone an owner.
- An admin cannot change or remove an owner.
- You cannot change your own role. Ask another owner to do it.
- The last owner cannot be moved to another role.

See Roles and permissions (topic `settings/roles-and-permissions`).

## Remove someone

Press **Remove** on their row and confirm. They lose the organization and every simulation in it. Their Mockzilla account stays, and so does everything they built.

The last owner cannot be removed. Make someone else an owner first.

## Sim collaborators

**Sim collaborators** at the top of the tab lists people who can reach some of your simulations without being in the organization. Filter the list by simulation to see who has which. **Remove** takes away all of their access to this organization.

See Share links (topic `simulations/share-links`).

## Invite someone

Open **Invitations** and press **Invite people**.

(Image: The invite dialog, with fields for the email address, the scope of the access and the role.)

*The invite dialog*

- **Email** is where the invitation goes.
- **Grant access** sets the scope: the whole organization, or the simulations you pick.
- **Role** sets what they can do once they are in.

The link in the email works for 7 days.

## Pending invitations

(Image: A pending invitation, with its role, its scope, the date it expires, and resend and revoke actions.)

*A pending invitation*

Every invitation you have sent is listed with its role, its scope and the day the link expires.

- **Resend** emails a new link and starts the 7 days again. The previous link stops working.
- **Revoke** cancels the invitation, and its link with it.

An invitation past its date reads **expired**. Resend it to give them another week.

## Invitations to you

**Received**, on the same tab, lists the organizations that have invited you. **Accept** puts you in with the role you were given. **Decline** clears the invitation.

## Seats

Your plan sets how many people the organization can hold, and how many of them can be owners or admins. Pending invitations take a seat too, so revoke the ones you no longer need.

Organizations without a paid plan cannot add anyone. The tab links to the plans that can.
